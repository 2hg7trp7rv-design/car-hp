# Editorial Minimal

Internal only

## Source lenses

- Magazine / Newspaper / Blog
- Typography
- Minimal
- Content architecture
- Responsive

## Visual signature

- First screen is led by one clear title
- Space separates meaning, not decorative boxes
- Large title, quiet lead, readable body
- Single column or restrained asymmetric editorial column
- Thin rules are preferred over cards
- Images support the subject only when needed
- Motion is absent or very subtle
- Navigation and footer stay light

## Recognition tokens

- Thin rules
- Typography as structure
- No outer card
- Quiet background
- Short link rows
- Low component count
- Editorial scroll rhythm

## CBJ use

Best for Guide articles, Column articles, Legal documents, Privacy, Disclaimer, Copyright, and Editorial policy

Use typographic hierarchy, thin dividers, definition-list summaries, and text links

Translate wide whitespace into Japanese-friendly reading rhythm

Avoid cards for document body, black related blocks, oversized footers, and decorative summary tiles
