## Visual signature

- Product clarity comes first
- Pricing, specs, filters, and actions are easy to scan
- Cards may be useful when items are genuinely comparable
- Image scale and product hierarchy need strict control
- Interaction should reduce decision effort

## Recognition tokens

- Product grid
- Filter rhythm
- Comparison rows
- Clear action path
- Strong product image
- Controlled specs

## CBJ use

Best for Cars listing, spec comparison, buying guide tables, and product-like article sections

Use only where comparison or selection is the actual task

Translate product clarity into car selection clarity

Avoid applying shopping-card logic to editorial documents or legal pages
