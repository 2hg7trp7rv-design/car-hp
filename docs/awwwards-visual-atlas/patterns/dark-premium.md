# Dark Premium

Internal only

## Source lenses

- Luxury
- Business & Corporate
- Photography
- Fullscreen
- Header Design
- Minimal

## Visual signature

- Black or near-black background carries authority
- One subject dominates the first view
- Typography is sharp, restrained, and low-noise
- Accent color is rare and deliberate
- Images need strong contrast and believable lighting
- Sections need breathing room, not stacked blocks
- CTA is small and controlled
- Footer must not become visually heavier than the page

## Recognition tokens

- Black field
- One strong subject
- Small labels
- Thin dividers
- Low CTA count
- Premium silence
- Controlled contrast

## CBJ use

Best for top pages, Cars feature pages, Heritage features, high-value guide hero areas, and selected landing pages

Use black background, restrained type, full-bleed or large image rhythm, and quiet navigation

Translate luxury pacing into automotive editorial confidence

Avoid black cards stacked inside light pages, excessive glow, fake premium gradients, and oversized CTA panels
