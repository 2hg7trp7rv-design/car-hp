## Visual signature

- Large type creates the visual identity
- Layout relies on scale, alignment, measure, and rhythm
- Images are secondary or absent
- Thin rules, numbering, and quiet labels organize sections
- The page should feel composed, not decorated
- Japanese text needs careful line breaks and moderate tracking

## Recognition tokens

- Oversized title
- Small eyebrow label
- Thin rule grid
- Controlled line length
- Low image dependency
- Strong baseline rhythm

## CBJ use

Best for Legal pages, About, Editorial policy, article intros, and high-authority statements

Use for pages where trust and clarity matter more than image impact

Translate dramatic Western typography into Japanese-readable hierarchy

Avoid unreadable microtype, over-tight tracking, split Japanese phrases, and type used only as decoration
