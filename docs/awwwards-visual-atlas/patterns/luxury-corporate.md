# Luxury Corporate

Internal only

## Source lenses

- Luxury
- Business & Corporate
- About Page
- Contact Page
- Navigation Menu

## Visual signature

- Calm authority
- Large but restrained typography
- Small labels
- Low number of sections
- Strong alignment
- Minimal CTA treatment
- Trust is built through order, not decoration

## Recognition tokens

- Quiet brand statement
- Profile table
- Sparse navigation
- Controlled black and white
- Minimal links
- Low ornament

## CBJ use

Best for About, operator profile, trust pages, contact pages, and legal hub pages

Use restraint, alignment, and clear authority

Translate corporate trust into automotive media credibility

Avoid generic company-site feature cards and overproduced SaaS-style blocks
