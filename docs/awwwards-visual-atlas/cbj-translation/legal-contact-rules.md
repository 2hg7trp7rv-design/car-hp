# CBJ Legal / Contact Visual Translation

Internal only

## Role

Legal and Contact pages are utility pages inside an editorial media site

They should feel trustworthy, quiet, and fast to understand

They should not feel like a component showcase

## Legal pages

### Use

- Small label
- Clear title
- Short lead
- Definition list for key facts
- Thin rules
- Text sections
- Compact related links
- Light footer

### Avoid

- Outer white card around the whole page
- Summary cards
- Related black feature blocks
- Dark documents card on mobile
- Repeated card stacks
- Overdesigned CTAs
- Full default footer if it visually dominates

### Page-specific translation

- Legal hub: compact link rows, not large cards
- Privacy: document layout with key facts table
- Disclaimer: document layout with section rules
- Copyright: document layout with section rules
- Ads: short policy document with a few key facts
- Editorial policy: process can be shown, but still light
- Sources: hierarchy can be shown, but not as heavy cards
- About: profile table and evidence, not many feature cards

## Contact page

### Use

- Contact title and short explanation
- Contact type as small list or tags
- Form appears quickly
- Note appears after the form
- Privacy link near form

### Avoid

- Big Contact hero pushing the form down
- Four large contact-type cards
- Note before form
- Default footer overpowering the form page
- Decorative black blocks

## Mobile order

Legal document:

1. Breadcrumb
2. Page label and title
3. Short lead
4. Key facts as a compact list
5. Document body
6. Related text links
7. Small documents links if needed
8. Light footer

Contact:

1. Breadcrumb
2. Contact title
3. Short explanation
4. Contact type list
5. Form
6. Note
7. Legal links
8. Light footer
