# CBJ Article Page Visual Translation

Internal only

## Role

Article pages are the core product of CBJ

The article must remain the main subject

Trust signals, related links, images, and CTAs are secondary

## Use

- Strong article title
- Natural lead
- One hero image if it helps the subject
- Clear reading width
- Chapter rhythm
- Thin section dividers
- Images only where they explain or strongly support the text
- Related links placed after the reader has context

## Avoid

- Trust badges that look like a separate section
- Oversized editorial review blocks
- Related cards interrupting article flow
- Decorative graphics that do not explain the article
- Huge cards in body copy
- Dense table blocks on mobile
- Generic conclusion modules

## Awwwards translation

Use award-level typography, rhythm, and spacing

Do not use award-site interaction if it weakens reading

Do not use visual novelty where the article needs clarity

## Mobile order

1. Category and breadcrumb if needed
2. Title
3. Lead
4. Hero image
5. Author and update information
6. Article body
7. Figures and tables only where useful
8. FAQ if it answers real search intent
9. Related links
10. Light trust or editorial note
