# CBJ Cars Visual Translation

Internal only

## Role

Cars pages need strong visual confidence, accurate vehicle identity, and clear scanning

They should feel premium and automotive

## Use

- Photo-led hero when the car deserves it
- Accurate vehicle shape
- Clear spec hierarchy
- Compact comparison rows
- Low-noise filters
- Quiet metadata
- Black or paper stage depending on context

## Avoid

- Product cards where editorial rows work better
- Generic stock-like car imagery
- Excessive glow
- Overly large filter panels
- Layouts that bury the car identity
- Hero text that covers important vehicle details

## Awwwards translation

Use photography-led hero discipline and luxury pacing

Translate product clarity into car selection clarity

Use interaction only where it reduces search effort

## Mobile order

1. Car or category identity
2. Strong image if useful
3. Key specs or filter summary
4. Main list or article body
5. Related cars or guides
6. Light footer
